Popular Destinations

Manali

Manali is a beautiful hill station located in the Indian state of Himachal Pradesh. It is famous for its mountains, snow-fall, and adventure sports. Tourists can enjoy activities like skiing, paragliding, and trekking in Manali.

Goa

Goa is a state in India known for its beautiful beaches, rich culture, and vibrant nightlife. It is a popular destination for both domestic and international tourists.
